About us

A studio rooted in meaningful memories

PicPitik is a small photography studio located in Nasugbu, Batangas. We provide photography services for birthdays, graduations, family events, and small businesses.

PicPitik started in 2024 with the goal of helping people save important memories through photographs. We believe every moment is worth remembering.
